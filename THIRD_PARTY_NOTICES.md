General Sans is designed by Indian Type Foundry and distributed through [Fontshare](https://www.fontshare.com/fonts/general-sans). It has its own ITF Free Font License and is not covered by this project's MIT License.

Font files are not included in this source release. The default local version uses the styled interface with its Arial/sans-serif fallback. If you want General Sans for your own project, obtain it directly from Fontshare and review its current terms.

An optional `BRAND_FONT=general-sans` build downloads the unmodified webfont files directly from Fontshare into the generated `dist/` output for use on the builder's own website. Do not add those files to your source repository or redistribute them as part of a template or font collection.
